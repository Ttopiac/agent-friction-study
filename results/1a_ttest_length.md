# t-test: dissatisfied replies after short vs long prompts

| Prompt length | n | M | SD |
|---|---|---|---|
| Short | 162 | 8.6 | 28.2 |
| Long | 148 | 8.8 | 28.4 |

## Result
Long prompts had more dissatisfied replies (*M* = 8.8%, *SD* = 28.4) than short prompts (*M* = 8.6%, *SD* = 28.2), but the difference was not significant, *t*(305.0) = 0.04, *p* = .965, *d* = 0.01.

## Meaning
Prompt length did not change how often the agent's work led to a dissatisfied reply. The cause of friction lies elsewhere.

## Limitation
Split at the median (17 words). Prompts from the same session are not independent, and the outcome is yes/no, so the t-test compares percentages only approximately.
