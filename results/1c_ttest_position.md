# t-test: dissatisfied replies after early vs late prompts in a session

| Position in session | n | M | SD |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early | 135 | 3.7 | 19.0 |
| Late | 149 | 14.1 | 34.9 |

## Result
Late prompts had more dissatisfied replies (*M* = 14.1%, *SD* = 34.9) than early prompts (*M* = 3.7%, *SD* = 19.0), and the difference was significant, *t*(232.8) = 3.16, *p* = .002, *d* = 0.36.

## Meaning
Friction builds up as a session goes on: prompts in the second half of a session were followed by a dissatisfied reply more often.

## Limitation
The link can run backwards: a session may become long because the agent failed early, not the other way around. Prompts from the same session are not independent, and the outcome is yes/no, so the t-test compares percentages only approximately.
