# Descriptive: dissatisfied messages by domain

| Domain | n | Dissatisfied | % |
|---|---|---|---|
| Web page | 19 | 4 | 21.1 |
| Slide deck | 160 | 28 | 17.5 |
| Unclassified | 21 | 1 | 4.8 |
| Documents & email | 28 | 1 | 3.6 |
| Admin forms (B) | 33 | 1 | 3.0 |
| Tools & setup | 38 | 1 | 2.6 |
| Admin forms (A) | 46 | 1 | 2.2 |
| Research & code | 24 | 0 | 0.0 |

## Result
Of 369 messages, 37 (10.0%) were dissatisfied. The rate was highest for Web page (21.1%) and Slide deck (17.5%), and at most 4.8% in every other domain.

## Meaning
Dissatisfaction was concentrated: Web page and Slide deck hold 86% of all dissatisfied messages, and 82% of their messages are about visual output. Other work, such as forms, documents, and code, rarely caused friction.

## Limitation
This is a description, not a test. Domains differ in size (19 to 160 messages), so percentages for small domains are unstable.
